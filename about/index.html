Our Story

About GeekBoZ.

Buying a gaming PC shouldn't feel like a gamble.

At GeekBoZ, we build high-performance systems with complete transparency — no shortcuts, no gimmicks, and no compromises. Every build is designed around what you actually need, not what gives us the highest margin.

Where It Started

Back in 2017, the PC market in Kerala had a problem. Gamers were often sold outdated or mismatched components, with little regard for performance or long-term value. We knew there had to be a better way.

So in Trivandrum, a small team of passionate gamers came together to start GeekBoZ — with one simple mission: Build honest, high-performance PCs for real users, and treat every customer like they matter.

What Makes GeekBoZ Different

We don't just assemble PCs — we build systems with purpose.

  • Carefully selected, high-quality components
  • Builds tailored to your exact use case
  • Honest recommendations — always performance-first
  • No pushy sales tactics, no confusing jargon

Built on Trust

Over the years, GeekBoZ has grown into one of Kerala's most recognized PC gaming brands — 1,000+ happy customers, 65,000+ YouTube subscribers, and 70,000+ followers across social platforms. But what truly sets us apart isn't just the numbers. It's the trust our community places in us.

Our Promise to You

We stand behind every system we build:

  • 3-Day 100% Return Policy
  • 30-Day Spot Replacement on any faulty component
  • 30-Day Upgrade Option — swap a component with zero depreciation

No fine print. No runaround. Just real support when you need it.

More Than Just a Store

What started as a single retail outlet has grown into something bigger. Today, GeekBoZ offers custom gaming PCs, workstations, and creator setups, online ordering with doorstep delivery, and a hands-on Experience Zone powered by ZOWIE — where you can try before you buy.

GeekBoZ isn't just a brand — it's a mindset. A belief that gamers and creators deserve better.
